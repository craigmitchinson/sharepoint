How much time will it take?
A tester gives two to four hours a week for six weeks; a hack day is one day; Frontier Friday is one hour. Agree it with your line manager first; we send them a short note explaining the commitment.
Do I need to be technical?
No. Most testers are colleagues who do the work every day. Frontier engineers do the building.
What do I get out of it?
Early hands-on time with new technology, your name on the result, and a say in what the Labs build next.
