How an experiment runs

Text web part
1 TO 2 WEEKS

Explore

Is the problem real, and what outcome would fix it? We talk to the people who have it, check what every Lab already runs, and only then choose what to test, whatever the technology.

Text web part
UP TO 6 WEEKS

Test

We build the smallest thing that answers the question and try it with colleagues on safe data, measuring against what happens today.

Text web part
1 WEEK

Decide

With the business owner: hand it to a Lab to build properly, stop, or test again with a change. We publish the result.

Text web part

Safe to try

  • Every experiment runs in the Frontier sandbox, never in production.
  • Synthetic or masked data unless Data Protection has signed off.
  • Agents prepare and colleagues decide, in tests as in live work.
  • Responsible AI review before any test that uses a language model with colleagues.
