Sizing

SizeWhat it means
XSA change to something already live
SA new step or object on a proven pattern
MA new process on platforms we already run
LA new process that needs a new integration
XLSeveral platforms, or a capability we don't have yet

No story points. No fixed dates. We commit to a size and a shape.

Text web part

Delivery shapes

Demoable. Working on real cases early; the business shapes it with us.

Incremental. Value in slices, each live and counted before the next.

Long-build. Value at the end; progress shown through gate evidence.

Picking a shape: ask when the business first needs to see it working, then pick the shape that gets them there.

Text web part

AI and decisions

Every AI use needs Responsible AI sign-off at F-grade before Ready to build.

Under the Data Use and Access Act, agents prepare and recommend; a colleague determines. Any design that automates a determination (for example, auto-redress) triggers Article 22C safeguards and needs Risk involved before Viable.

Code or release files leave our estate only through the sanitiser and with Information Security's written ruling.
