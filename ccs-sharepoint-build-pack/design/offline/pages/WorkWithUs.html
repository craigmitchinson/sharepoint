Which route do I pick?
If it already exists and is wrong, report a problem. If it exists and you want it different, or something it depends on is changing, change something. If it doesn't exist yet, bring us something new. If you're not sure, ask a question and we'll route it.
What makes a good candidate for something new?
High volume, rules you can write down, and data that arrives electronically. If every case needs judgement, an agent may be able to prepare it for your colleagues instead.
A system one of your automations uses is changing. What do you need from us?
Choose Change something, then "A system it uses is changing". We assess the impact on every automation that touches it and tell you what needs to change and when.
Will it cost my area anything?
No. Build and run are funded centrally by Wealth and Insurance for 2026, and we will tell you before that changes.
