How we work at Frontier

01

Start with the problem and the outcome

Every experiment begins with a problem we can describe in a sentence and the outcome we want: a better service, a quicker process, less risk. The technology comes last.

02

Check what we already have

Before we build anything we look across every Lab. If CCS already runs something that fits, we point you to it or extend it rather than build it twice.

03

Test small, measure, publish

The smallest thing that answers the question, tried with the people who do the work and measured against today. Stopping is a good result; we publish what we learned either way.
