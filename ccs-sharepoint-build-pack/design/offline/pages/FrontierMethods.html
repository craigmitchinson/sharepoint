Methods

New ways to solve old problems.

Text web part · lead paragraph

The methods we use to test ideas quickly and safely. Every one is free to use in your own team; ask a Frontier champion to run one with you the first time.

Three-column sections × 2 · a Text web part in each
1 HOUR

Problem framing

Before anything else: who has the problem, how often, and what good looks like.

5 DAYS

Design sprint

A big problem, a cross-team group, a tested prototype by Friday.

1 TO 2 WEEKS

Fake it first

A person plays the part of the technology behind the scenes, to see if the idea is worth building.

2 TO 4 WEEKS

Shadow testing

The new thing runs alongside real work, unseen, and we compare its answers with what actually happened.

DAYS

Synthetic data

Realistic but invented data, so we can test without touching a real customer record.

ONGOING

Pairing with the people who do the work

Builders sit with colleagues while they work, so we solve the real problem, not the described one.

Technology radar

We choose the technology to fit the problem; the radar shows what is ready, not what we must use. Adopt means we use it in live work. Trial means it is proven in experiments and ready for a Lab to pick up. Assess means worth exploring. Hold means not now, with the reason on the radar page.
