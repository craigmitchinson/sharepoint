Voice notes to case summaries

Text web part · lead paragraph

Claims handlers dictate a short voice note after each call, and a language model drafts the case summary for them to check and save.

Text web part

The problem

Handlers spend around nine minutes after each call writing the case summary, and summaries vary in what they include. Across the claims team that is about 1,400 hours a month.

What we are testing

If handlers dictate a voice note and the model drafts the summary, will a summary take under three minutes, and will handlers accept at least eight in ten with only small edits?

What it does not do

It does not decide anything about the claim. The handler checks, edits and saves every summary.

What we have learned so far

2 Oct
Week 4: 84% of drafts accepted with small edits, above the 80% target. Edits are mostly policy numbers read out wrongly; we are testing reading them back to the handler.
18 Sep
Week 2: handlers preferred shorter voice notes than we expected. We changed the prompt to work from 20 seconds of speech.
8 Sep
Started with six handlers from the motor claims team, on synthetic call recordings in the Frontier sandbox.
