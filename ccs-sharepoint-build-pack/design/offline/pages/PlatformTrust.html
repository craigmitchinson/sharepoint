Trust and controls · every lab

Safe to rely on, and easy to evidence.

Strong section · Text web part
Agents prepare. People decide.

Under the Data Use and Access Act, decisions with a significant effect on a customer cannot be made by automation alone. Our agents gather the facts, check them against our own data and prepare the case. A colleague makes the determination, and the record shows both.

We design to this from the start. If a design would let an automation decide, it does not pass our first gate.

How every automation is controlled

Text web part

Signed off before build

Every use of AI goes through Responsible AI review before build starts. Every automation passes six gates, each needing evidence, before it goes live.

Text web part

Recorded as it runs

Every action is logged with its inputs and outputs. For agents, the colleague's decision is recorded alongside the agent's recommendation.

Text web part

Watched once it's live

Our Service Team monitors every automation and its telemetry, so we usually spot a problem before your teams do.

Can an agent make a decision about a customer?
No. Agents prepare and recommend. A colleague decides, and both are recorded.
Who owns an automation once it's live?
The business area owns the process. Our Service Team owns keeping the automation running and its value measured.
What happens when a system it uses changes?
Monitoring flags it, the Service Team fixes it, and the change goes through the same controls as new work.
