How we count value

Value is colleague time returned: live case volumes multiplied by the time each case took by hand, converted at the blended colleague rate.

Run cost is everything it takes to keep the estate live, including our people, licences and infrastructure.

Cost per case is run cost divided by cases completed. FTE is full-time equivalent: one colleague's working year.

Text web part

Why it lasts

Our Service Team runs every automation after it goes live. When the systems around it change, they fix it. That is why value reported last year is still true this year, and why we report it as a running total rather than a one-off saving.
